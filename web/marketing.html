BEFORE YOU GO

Can I already try SendRack?

The pilot app is available through “Open the app”. Service availability depends on your location and the routes configured by the operator. The waitlist keeps you informed.

Do my customers need an account?

No. You can share a private tracking link so they can view delivery progress in their browser.

What happens after I join?

Your details are saved on the SendRack waitlist. We’ll use your email for SendRack updates. Joining does not create an app account or book a delivery.
