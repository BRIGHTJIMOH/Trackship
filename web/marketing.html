01 — OUR VISION

A clearer journey.
A more connected Nigeria.

Our vision is a Nigeria where doorpost-to-doorpost delivery connects businesses and communities, while creating earning opportunities for the people who move parcels.

↗

OUR MISSION

Connect deliveries.
Create opportunities.

To connect vendors with verified riders and drivers for doorpost-to-doorpost parcel delivery, make each handoff easier to follow, and open up delivery earning opportunities for eligible vehicle owners.

OUR VALUE PROPOSITION

Verified handlers.
Doorpost to doorpost.

For vendors, SendRack brings together verified handlers, shipment details and delivery updates for the journey from doorpost to doorpost. For commercial riders and eligible private car or motorcycle owners, it offers a way to access delivery opportunities and earn extra income when jobs are available.
