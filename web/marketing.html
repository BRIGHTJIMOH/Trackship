01 — OUR VISION

Thriving businesses.
Stronger livelihoods.

A Nigeria where every vendor can build a thriving business from their doorstep, and more people can earn sustainable livelihoods by connecting businesses, customers and communities.

↗

OUR MISSION

Remove barriers.
Open possibilities.

To remove the delivery barriers that hold small businesses back by connecting vendors with verified handlers through accountable, technology-enabled logistics. We work to expand earning opportunities for riders and vehicle owners, while building partnerships that improve access to business skills, markets and productive resources.

OUR VALUE PROPOSITION

Know who handles it.
Follow its journey.

SendRack brings doorpost-to-doorpost delivery, verified handlers and shipment visibility into one platform—helping vendors serve customers beyond their immediate location while keeping oversight of their parcels. For riders and eligible vehicle owners, it provides access to delivery opportunities that can turn their mobility into an additional source of income.

OUR COMMITMENT TO ENTERPRISE AND LIVELIHOODS

Stronger businesses.
More ways to earn.

Our ambition extends beyond moving parcels. As SendRack grows, we aim to help more young people, women and small business owners access the knowledge, tools and opportunities they need to participate in commerce and strengthen their livelihoods.

Delivery is where we begin.

Our immediate focus is a platform that makes parcel handling accountable, connects vendors with verified handlers and gives riders and drivers access to delivery work. We are inviting early participants to help us shape the experience.

Enterprise is where we want to make a difference.

Over time, we intend to develop partnerships that support practical business education, access to selling opportunities and productive equipment. These are plans for growth, guided by the needs of the people using SendRack.
