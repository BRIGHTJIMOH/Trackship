LOCATION
A small card appears after signing into the Android app. Turn on enables location use after phone Location and permission are ready. Android requires the user to turn on device Location in the settings panel if it is off; apps cannot silently flip this system switch. The app opens the relevant panel. If permission has been denied, Phone location settings opens app settings when appropriate.

Turn off in app pauses SendRack location access, clears the foreground viewer request and stops the delivery-location service. The preference is retained on the device. It does not turn off GPS for other apps, disable mobile internet, change custody, or cancel a delivery. Rider automatic tracking respects the pause and does not restart until enabled. Vendors do not start rider background tracking. While off, customers will not receive fresh rider positions; the existing server freshness checks still apply if the phone is offline when stopping.

Reopen the small card from Profile > Appearance & location > Location settings. The existing PIN lock may require sign-in when returning from phone Settings; this update does not bypass that protection. A location setting choice is distinct from a shipment's pickup address or manually confirmed pickup pin. The card does not fabricate a location fix or silently grant phone permissions.
